SYNTHETIC FINANCIAL RESEARCH NOTE
Title: AsterCloud FY2027 Capital Plan and Project Atlas Update
Date: 2026-06-30
Source: Northstar Research, fictional training corpus

Company profile
AsterCloud is a fictional hyperscale cloud provider with enterprise infrastructure, data-platform,
and AI-services businesses. The company generates stable cash flow from legacy hosting and database
products, while its faster-growing AI services remain investment intensive. Management reports that
large enterprise customers are testing AI workloads but remain selective about multi-year commitments.

FY2027 capital plan
AsterCloud plans FY2027 capital expenditure of 48 billion dollars, including 19 billion dollars for
the NorthGrid data-centre programme. NorthGrid requires new transmission equipment, long-term power
purchase agreements, and high-density cooling before all planned capacity can be commissioned. The
company expects the first NorthGrid campus to accept customer workloads in the fourth quarter of 2027.
Delays in power interconnection would push depreciation expense ahead of revenue recognition.

Project Atlas monetisation
Project Atlas is AsterCloud's managed inference and model-hosting platform. Its initial pricing uses
reserved capacity and consumption charges. Management expects Atlas utilisation to reach 65 percent by
the end of FY2027, but current customer pilots have not yet converted into contracted production
workloads. If Atlas monetisation slips, the NorthGrid programme would have a longer payback period,
weaker free-cash-flow conversion, and greater valuation sensitivity to real yields.

What would change the outlook
Evidence of signed enterprise commitments, contracted NorthGrid power, and rising Atlas utilisation
would support the investment case. A capex reduction, a power-connection delay, or Atlas utilisation
below the 65 percent target would increase the risk of earnings-estimate revisions.
